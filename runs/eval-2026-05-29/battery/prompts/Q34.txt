In this little config module (config.mjs + merge.mjs), `loadConfig(user)` is meant to layer the user's settings on top of the built-in DEFAULTS, with nested objects merged rather than replaced. Two things are broken: the user's settings are being ignored (defaults win), and nested objects get clobbered wholesale so setting one nested field wipes out its siblings. For example loadConfig({ server: { port: 9090 } }) should keep server.host as "localhost" while changing the port to 9090, and loadConfig({ debug: true }) should turn debug on. Fix it. You can check the basics with `node test_basic.mjs`; keep it passing.
