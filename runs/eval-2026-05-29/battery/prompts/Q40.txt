Implement the `EventEmitter` class in solution.ts. It should support: `on(event, listener)` to register a listener; `emit(event, ...args)` to call every listener registered for that event, in the order they were added, passing the args along; `off(event, listener)` to remove a specific listener; and `once(event, listener)` to register a listener that fires at most once and then removes itself. Emitting an event with no listeners should simply do nothing. You can check the basics with `node test_basic.ts`; keep it passing.
