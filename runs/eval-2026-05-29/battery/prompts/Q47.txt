The script solution.sh integer-divides its first argument by its second and prints the result. The division-by-zero handling is wrong: when the second argument is 0 it currently just prints "Infinity" to stdout and exits successfully, which hides the error from callers. Fix it so a zero divisor instead prints an error message to stderr, exits with a non-zero status, and prints nothing to stdout — while normal division still works (e.g. bash solution.sh 10 2 prints 5).
