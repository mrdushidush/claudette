Implement `parseCsv(text)` in solution.mjs — a parser for multi-line CSV text that returns an array of rows, each row an array of field strings. It needs to handle real CSV properly: fields may be wrapped in double quotes, a quoted field can contain commas AND newlines, and a doubled "" inside a quoted field is a literal double-quote. Lines may be separated by "\n" or "\r\n". A single trailing newline at the end of the input should not produce an extra empty row. For example parseCsv('a,"b,c",d') is [["a","b,c","d"]]. You can check the basics with `node test_basic.mjs`; keep it passing.
