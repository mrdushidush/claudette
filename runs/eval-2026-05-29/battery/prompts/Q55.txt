Implement `mergeIntervals(intervals)` in solution.ts. Given a list of [start, end] number intervals, merge all overlapping intervals and return the result sorted by start. Two intervals that merely touch at an endpoint (like [1,2] and [2,3]) count as overlapping and should be merged into one ([1,3]). The input is not necessarily sorted, and one interval may be fully contained in another. For example [[1,3],[2,4]] merges to [[1,4]]. You can check the basics with `node test_basic.ts`; keep it passing.
